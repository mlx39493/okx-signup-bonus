## What tasks may be required before the reward is unlocked?

The exact task list depends on the campaign attached to your account. Common requirements can include the following.

### Identity verification

Most regulated crypto exchange promotions require identity verification. This may include personal details, an identity document, and additional checks depending on location and account activity.

Completing verification does not automatically guarantee a reward. It may only make the account eligible to see or participate in a promotion.

### Deposit requirement

Some campaigns require a minimum deposit. The amount may be measured as a net deposit, which can account for deposits minus withdrawals. Internal transfers may not qualify under certain campaign rules.

Before transferring funds, check:

- The minimum amount
- Whether fiat or crypto deposits qualify
- Whether the deposit must remain in the account
- Whether the deposit must arrive before a deadline
- Whether transfers from another OKX account are excluded

A deposit is not the same as a guaranteed return. The funds may still be exposed to crypto price movements, withdrawal rules, and trading fees.

### First qualifying trade

Other promotions require a first trade or a minimum trading volume. OKX’s published terms distinguish qualifying trades from excluded transactions. Certain stablecoin conversions and zero-fee pairs may not count toward the required trading amount.

That point is easy to miss. If a promotion requires a qualifying trade, do not assume that every conversion inside the app will count. Open the reward task and check the eligible product, order type, minimum size, and deadline first.

Rewards may also have a lock-up period before they can be traded or withdrawn. OKX’s referral documentation describes a seven-day lock-up for certain referral rewards, while other new-user programs can have different restrictions.
